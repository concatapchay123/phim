# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
- Movie enthusiasts looking for a premium, clean, and distraction-free environment to watch movies online.
- Users who want to share a cozy, "chill" movie-watching vibe with friends or watch together.
- Visitors seeking high-quality Vietsub/Thuyết Minh movies with fast loading and zero annoying ads.

## Product Purpose
- To provide a premium movie streaming experience named **Quán Chill** (formerly Phim Lợn) that evokes the cozy, immersive feeling of a modern cinema lounge.
- To make browsing and discovering movies comfortable, visual, and seamless.
- To express a relaxed, comfortable mood with light costume-drama character through seal-inspired brand details.

## Brand Personality
- Light costume-drama character through seal-like brand details, not heavy historical decoration.
- Cozy (Ấm cúng)
- Cinematic (Đậm chất điện ảnh)
- Premium (Cao cấp)
- Warm-dark (Tông tối ấm áp)
- Chill (Thư thái)

## Anti-references
- Saturated AI-purple gradients.
- Generic three-column feature cards with flat icons.
- Diagonal gradient background stripes or grid line overlays.
- Ghost cards (1px border combined with a large blurred shadow).
- Centered hero over dark mesh.
- Crowded interfaces filled with banners and spam popups.
- Overly modern tech-glass branding that loses the relaxed, old-world seal identity.

## Design Principles
1. **Theater Mode Default**: High visual contrast focusing on the movie content, using warm gold, deep vermilion, and dark tones to feel cinematic without becoming harsh.
2. **Space to Breathe**: Generous margins, clean alignments, and asymmetric layout variance to give the user a premium magazine feel.
3. **Motion is Emotion**: Meaningful, smooth page transitions and card-hover reveals using hardware-accelerated GSAP animations.
4. **No Placeholders**: Real movie posters and backdrops, clean custom SVGs, and a distinctive seal-inspired QuÃ¡n Chill brand mark.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio $\ge 4.5:1$ for body copy and placeholder text.
- Supports prefers-reduced-motion media query to bypass GSAP animations.
- Semantically structured HTML headings ($H1$ to $H3$).
